About AeroTrace

AeroTrace is an industrial pollution source attribution platform.

Tracing pollution to its probable industrial sources using analytical methods, geospatial context, and sensor data.

Scientific Limitations & Disclaimer

This product provides analytical attribution based on available evidence. It does not provide legal determination of guilt or causation. "Attribution Score" represents relative evidence strength, not strict scientific probability. Simplified trajectories are not regulatory-grade dispersion models.
